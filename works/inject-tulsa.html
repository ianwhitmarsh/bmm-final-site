Portfolio Description

Inject Tulsa's social media presence is strategically developed to elevate brand awareness, increase engagement, and position the practice as a trusted destination for aesthetic treatments. Through a consistent, results-driven approach, we transform their social platforms into a polished, high-performing digital presence that drives both visibility and client interest. Our content strategy blends educational, promotional, and lifestyle-driven content to reflect the brand's expertise while keeping the tone approachable and engaging. By leveraging short-form video, treatment education, and visually compelling content, we create a social presence that not only attracts attention but builds trust with potential clients.

How We Started

Inject Tulsa comes to us looking to grow their online presence and better connect with their audience through social media. While offering high-quality aesthetic services, their digital platforms lacked consistency and strategic direction. Our goal was to develop a cohesive content strategy that: increased visibility and reach; improved engagement and interaction; positions Inject Tulsa as a trusted, go-to provider; turns social media into a consistent lead-generation channel.

Content Strategy & Planning

We begin by developing a structured content strategy tailored to Inject Tulsa's audience and services. Each month includes a Monthly Strategy Sheet and content planning session to map out themes such as treatment education, client results, promotions, and trending content. This ensures every post serves a purpose—whether it is building trust, increasing awareness, or driving conversions.

Content Creation & Posting

We implement a consistent posting schedule focused on short-form video (reels) and high-performing visual content. Content includes: before-and-after transformations; treatment education and FAQs; client-focused messaging; trend-based and relatable content; promotional and seasonal offers. Each post is paired with custom-written copy designed to reflect Inject Tulsa's brand voice while encouraging engagement and action.

Platform Optimization

We optimize both Instagram and Facebook to ensure content performs effectively across platforms. This includes: structuring content for maximum reach and retention; leveraging platform-specific trends and formats; maintaining consistent branding and messaging; encouraging interaction through captions and calls-to-action.

Ongoing Performance Optimization

Through weekly check-ins and ongoing analysis, we continuously refine the content strategy based on performance. High-performing content types are scaled, while underperforming approaches are adjusted to improve results over time.

Key Features

Short-Form Video Strategy: a strong focus on reels and video content to maximize reach, engagement, and discoverability. Educational + Trust-Building Content: clear, informative posts that help potential clients understand treatments and feel confident booking. Before & After Visual Storytelling: transformation-based content that showcases real results and builds credibility. Trend Integration: strategic use of trending formats and audio to increase visibility and relevance. Consistent Brand Voice: custom copywriting that balances professionalism with approachability. Multi-Platform Growth Strategy: coordinated posting and optimization across Instagram and Facebook to maximize overall performance.

Results

The impact of the strategy is both immediate and sustained: 51% increase in Instagram impressions (14.11k) within the first month; 82% increase in Instagram interactions within the first month; 20% sustained increase in engagement over the following three months; 40% increase in Facebook engagement over three months; 20.87k Facebook views (600% increase) over three months; 46.55k Instagram views (20.9% increase) over three months. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of a consistent, strategic content approach, turning Inject Tulsa's social media into a powerful tool for brand growth, audience engagement, and client acquisition.
