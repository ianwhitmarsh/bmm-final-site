Project Overview

Butler Homes is a premier custom home builder serving Tulsa, Broken Arrow, Bixby, Jenks, Owasso, and surrounding Oklahoma communities. With over 25 years of experience in the homebuilding industry, Butler Homes has built a reputation for delivering exceptional craftsmanship, innovative floor plans, and an outstanding customer experience. Their portfolio includes custom homes, semi-custom homes, quick move-in properties, and build-on-your-land opportunities, providing homeowners with flexible solutions tailored to their needs. As their company continued to grow, Butler Homes needed a website that not only reflected the quality of their homes but also served as a powerful business tool capable of supporting future expansion. Their existing process for updating website content was time-consuming and often required developer involvement, creating delays whenever new homes, communities, promotions, or floor plans needed to be added.

The Challenge

The primary challenge was to create a website that matched the premium quality of the Butler Homes brand while providing a modern user experience for prospective homebuyers. The website needed to showcase homes, communities, floor plans, and available inventory in a visually compelling way while remaining fast, mobile-friendly, and easy to navigate. Equally important was creating a backend management system that empowered the Butler Homes team to make updates independently. Rather than relying on a developer for every change, they wanted the ability to quickly add new communities, update available homes, modify floor plans, publish promotions, and manage content as their business evolved. The solution needed to balance beautiful design with practical functionality, giving both customers and administrators an exceptional experience.

Our Solution

We designed and developed a fully custom website that combines modern aesthetics with powerful functionality. Every aspect of the site was built to highlight the craftsmanship and attention to detail that Butler Homes is known for. The website features: a modern, responsive design optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices; high-quality imagery and visual storytelling that showcases completed homes and communities; community pages that provide visitors with detailed information about available neighborhoods and lots; floor plan galleries that allow prospective buyers to explore available home designs; inventory home listings that can be updated and managed internally; strategic calls-to-action that encourage visitors to schedule consultations and request information; performance-focused development to ensure fast load times and an excellent user experience.

Custom Admin Panel

One of the most valuable components of the project was the development of a custom administrative dashboard designed specifically around Butler Homes' workflow. The admin panel allows their team to: add and manage available homes; update floor plans and specifications; create and edit community information; publish special offers and promotions; upload new images and project galleries; modify website content without technical knowledge; maintain complete control over website updates in real time. This eliminates the bottleneck of waiting on developers to make simple changes and gives the Butler Homes team the flexibility to keep their website current as inventory and promotions change.

The Results

The finished website provides Butler Homes with a scalable digital platform that supports both marketing and operational efficiency. Prospective homebuyers can easily browse homes, explore communities, and learn about the building process, while the Butler Homes team can confidently manage and update content whenever needed. By combining premium design, modern technology, and a custom-built content management system, the new website positions Butler Homes for continued growth while ensuring their online presence reflects the same level of quality and professionalism found in every home they build. The result is more than just a website—it is a long-term business asset that gives Butler Homes complete control over their digital presence while providing an exceptional experience for future homeowners.
