Website Design + Development

Jacob & Associates Realty (JA Realtors)

4 Weeks completion timeline

Project Overview

JA Realtors' website was designed to function as a comprehensive real estate platform that allows buyers and sellers to explore listings, research communities, and connect directly with experienced agents. The site combines a clean, user-friendly design with powerful property search tools, making it easy for visitors to browse homes, estimate property values, and begin the buying or selling process.

Discovery & Industry Strategy

We began by understanding the unique requirements of a real estate website. Unlike many service-based sites, real estate platforms must handle constantly changing property listings, advanced search tools, and multiple user pathways for both buyers and sellers. Our strategy focused on building a site that would act as both a property discovery platform and a lead-generation tool for the brokerage.

Website Architecture & Listing Structure

The site architecture was designed to organize real estate information in a way that feels intuitive for home buyers and sellers. Key sections include: featured listings and property search tools; advanced home search and browse-by-city options; community pages for popular Northeast Oklahoma areas; selling resources such as property value estimators; agent profiles and brokerage information. This structure allows visitors to quickly move between researching homes, exploring communities, and connecting with agents for assistance.

MLS Integration & Dynamic Property Search

A key component of the site is its MLS-powered property search system, which allows visitors to browse available homes across multiple Oklahoma markets. Users can: search properties by city; filter listings by price and features; view featured listings; calculate affordability and mortgage estimates. These tools transform the website into a dynamic real estate search platform rather than a static marketing site, providing real value to users actively searching for homes.

UI/UX Design

The design emphasizes clarity and ease of navigation while handling large amounts of property data. Key UX considerations included: simple navigation for buyers and sellers; clear listing presentation with strong imagery; structured filters for property searches; easy pathways to contact agents. The goal was to ensure users could quickly browse homes and take the next step toward scheduling showings or requesting more information.

Development & Mobile Optimization

Because many home buyers browse listings from their phones, the site was built with fully responsive design to ensure a seamless mobile experience. Performance optimization and structured property data ensure listings load quickly while maintaining smooth navigation across devices.

Launch & Digital Impact

Following launch, the website now serves as a central digital hub for JA Realtors, allowing the brokerage to showcase listings, connect with potential buyers and sellers, and provide real estate resources to the communities they serve. The combination of dynamic listing technology, clear navigation, and strong community focus helps position JA Realtors as a trusted real estate resource throughout Northeast Oklahoma.

Key Features

MLS-Powered Property Search: integrated real estate listing technology allows visitors to search active homes across multiple Oklahoma markets. Advanced Search Tools: buyers can filter properties by location, price range, and features while browsing homes throughout the region. Community-Focused Real Estate Pages: dedicated sections highlight popular markets such as Tulsa, Bixby, Jenks, Broken Arrow, Owasso, and Coweta. Buyer & Seller Resources: tools such as affordability calculators and property value estimators support both sides of the real estate process. Agent Showcase: the platform highlights the brokerage's team of agents, helping clients easily connect with experienced professionals. Mobile-Optimized Listing Experience: responsive design ensures property browsing and home searches work smoothly across phones, tablets, and desktop devices.
