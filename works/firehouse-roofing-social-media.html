Main Description

Firehouse Roofing's social media presence was designed to balance professionalism with personality, using approachable, lighthearted content to highlight their roofing expertise, connect with homeowners, and make a typically serious service feel friendly, relatable, and trustworthy.

Content Planning & Strategy

We worked with Firehouse Roofing to develop a social media strategy that felt informative without being stiff. Each month, we create a Monthly Strategy Sheet and hold a Monthly Content Session to plan content around educational moments, behind-the-scenes clips, team highlights, and seasonal roofing tips—always with a touch of humor and personality.

Content Creation & Posting

To keep content fresh and engaging, we produce 3 reels and 1 static post per week for Instagram and Facebook. Posts are paired with custom-written copy that maintains Firehouse Roofing's upbeat, approachable voice, helping the brand stand out while still clearly communicating expertise.

Campaign Support & Promotion

Seasonal promotions and service highlights are integrated naturally into the content calendar, ensuring marketing moments feel organic rather than sales-driven. This approach allows Firehouse Roofing to stay top of mind while building genuine audience connection.

Performance Tracking & Reporting

We provide Quarterly Social Media Reports to review growth, content engagement, and audience engagement. These insights helped guide future content decisions and ensured the strategy continued to resonate with Firehouse Roofing's community.
