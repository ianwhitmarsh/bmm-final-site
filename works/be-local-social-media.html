Project Description

BeLocal Tulsa's social media presence was designed to spotlight the people, places, and stories that make Tulsa unique, creating a dynamic and engaging digital platform that connects local businesses, residents, and newcomers through curated content and community-driven storytelling.

Content Planning & Strategy

We partnered with BeLocal Tulsa to create a cohesive social media strategy that reflected the magazine's mission of celebrating local culture and connection. Each month, we developed a Monthly Strategy Sheet and led a Monthly Content Session to plan content themes including local features, business spotlights, community events, and editorial highlights.

Content Creation & Posting

To maintain a consistent and engaging publishing schedule, we produce weekly content across Instagram and Facebook. Every post was supported by custom-written copy tailored to BeLocal Tulsa's editorial voice—informative, welcoming, and community-focused—ensuring content felt both curated and authentic.

Performance Tracking & Reporting

To guide growth and optimize performance, we delivered Quarterly Social Media & Ad Reports outlining audience growth, content performance, engagement trends, and ad results. These insights informed future content decisions and ensured BeLocal Tulsa's digital presence continued to evolve alongside its community.
