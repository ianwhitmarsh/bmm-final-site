Main Description

Luedke Lights & Lawncare's digital presence was designed to reflect their upbeat personality and reliable services, pairing a clean, easy-to-navigate website with playful, engaging social media content that showcases their work while connecting with their local community in a fun, approachable way.

Discovery & Brand Direction

We kicked off the project by defining Luedke Lights & Lawncare's brand personality—professional, dependable, and fun. The goal was to create a digital experience that felt friendly and approachable while clearly communicating their services and making it easy for customers to get in touch.

Website Strategy & Development

The website was structured to be simple, clear, and conversion-focused, allowing visitors to quickly understand services, view past work, and request quotes. Clean layouts, bold headings, and straightforward navigation ensured a smooth experience across all devices.

UI/UX Design

We designed the site with a bright, welcoming aesthetic that matched the brand's energetic tone. Visual elements and spacing were intentionally kept light and uncluttered, creating a user-friendly experience that feels both modern and approachable.

Social Media Content & Posting

To bring the brand's personality to life online, we created social media content that leaned into humor, seasonal moments, and behind-the-scenes looks at the team in action. Posts were crafted to be engaging and relatable, balancing service promotion with playful storytelling that resonated with their local audience.

Launch & Ongoing Growth

After final testing and launch, both the website and social channels were positioned to support ongoing growth. The combined strategy resulted in a cohesive digital presence that highlights Luedke Lights & Lawncare's services while making the brand feel human, fun, and easy to work with.
