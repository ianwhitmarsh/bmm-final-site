Red Brick Pizza

Red Brick Pizza's elevated social media presence was crafted to showcase their fresh ingredients, signature fire-baked flavors, and community-centered spirit, creating an engaging digital experience that drives appetite, awareness, and customer connection across platforms.

Content Planning & Strategy

Our collaboration with Red Brick Pizza began with a clear focus: build a social media presence that reflects their handcrafted flavors and strong community roots. Each month, we developed a detailed Monthly Strategy Sheet and hosted a Monthly Content Session to align on themes, promotions, and messaging that would resonate with their audience across platforms.

Content Creation & Posting

To maintain consistency and drive engagement, we produced weekly content tailored specifically for Instagram and Facebook. Every asset included custom copy crafted to fit Red Brick Pizza's brand voice, ensuring cohesive storytelling across every channel. Our weekly check-ins kept content timely, relevant, and aligned with ongoing promotions or menu highlights.

Performance Tracking & Reporting

To ensure transparency and continuous improvement, we delivered Social Media & Ad Reports. These reports analyzed performance insights, audience trends, content effectiveness, and campaign results. Data from these reports guided future strategies and allowed us to refine our approach for maximum impact.
