Project Description

Lee Signature Homes' digital presence was designed to showcase their dedication to craftsmanship, thoughtful design, and custom home excellence.

Discovery & Brand Direction

We began by understanding Lee Signature Homes' building philosophy, target audience, and design standards. Our goal was to create a digital presence that reflected quality, professionalism, and the personalized nature of their custom home-building process.

Website Strategy & Development

The website was strategically structured to highlight completed projects, services, and the Lee Signature Homes process. Clear navigation, strong calls-to-action, and organized content guide visitors through the site, making it easy to explore homes, understand services, and inquire about future builds.

UI/UX Design

We designed a clean, elevated website experience using refined typography, balanced layouts, and intentional visual hierarchy. High-quality imagery was prioritized to showcase craftsmanship, while responsive development ensured a seamless experience across all devices.

Social Media Strategy & Content Creation

On social media, we developed a content strategy focused on showcasing projects, behind-the-scenes progress, design details, and educational insights. Posts were paired with custom-written copy that reinforced credibility while maintaining an approachable and professional tone.

Launch & Ongoing Growth

Following launch, both the website and social channels were positioned for continued growth. Ongoing content insights and performance reviews help refine strategy and maintain a consistent, high-quality digital presence for Lee Signature Homes.
