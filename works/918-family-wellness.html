Main Description

918 Family Wellness's digital presence is strategically developed to reflect their family-centered approach to care while increasing visibility, engagement, and community connection. By combining a welcoming, easy-to-navigate website with a consistent, results-driven social media strategy, we create a cohesive online experience that educates, engages, and builds trust with families.

How We Started

918 Family Wellness comes to us looking to strengthen both their online presence and patient experience. While offering valuable services, they need a more cohesive digital strategy—both through a professional website and consistent social media content. Our goal is to create a unified digital ecosystem that: increases local visibility and awareness; builds trust with families and parents; improves engagement across social platforms; provides a seamless, user-friendly website experience; supports patient inquiries and appointment conversions.

Website Strategy & Development

We begin by building a website that reflects the warm, family-friendly nature of the practice while prioritizing usability and clarity. The site is structured to: clearly present services and care offerings; provide an easy, intuitive navigation experience for all age groups; guide visitors toward scheduling appointments or contacting the office; create a welcoming digital environment that reflects in-office care. Clean layouts, accessible design, and clear calls-to-action ensure that families can quickly find the information they need and take the next step with confidence.

Content Strategy & Planning

Alongside the website, we develop a monthly social media strategy tailored to 918 Family Wellness's audience. Each month includes a Monthly Strategy Sheet and planning session focused on wellness education, family care, seasonal topics, and community engagement. This ensures consistency across platforms and alignment with the brand's messaging.

Content Creation & Posting

We implement a consistent posting schedule across Instagram and Facebook, focusing on content that is both engaging and informative. Content includes: family-focused wellness education; relatable and engaging lifestyle content; patient education and FAQs; seasonal and community-driven posts; brand personality and team highlights. Each post is paired with custom-written copy that reflects a warm, approachable tone and encourages interaction.

Platform Optimization

We optimize content across both platforms to maximize performance and reach. This includes: structuring content for visibility and engagement; leveraging platform-specific formats and trends; maintaining consistent branding and messaging; encouraging interaction through captions and calls-to-action.

Ongoing Performance Optimization

Through continuous monitoring and analysis, we refine both content and strategy over time. High-performing content is scaled, and adjustments are made to improve engagement, reach, and audience growth across platforms.

Key Features

Family-Friendly Website Experience: a clean, welcoming website designed for easy navigation and a positive user experience for all age groups. Conversion-Focused Design: strategic calls-to-action and clear pathways guide visitors toward contacting the practice or scheduling appointments. Educational & Trust-Building Content: both the website and social platforms provide valuable information that builds confidence with patients. Consistent Brand Voice: warm, approachable messaging across all platforms reflects the practice's family-centered values. Multi-Platform Growth Strategy: coordinated content across Instagram and Facebook ensures consistent visibility and engagement. Community-Centered Content: content designed to resonate with local families and strengthen community connection.

Results

The combined website and social strategy drives strong, measurable growth across platforms: 261.1K Facebook impressions (125% increase) over the past three months; 44.46K Instagram impressions (11.95% increase) over the past three months; 305.58K total impressions across both platforms (162% increase); 14.22K total interactions (148% increase), including 13.45K on Facebook (137% increase) and 774 on Instagram (1,700% increase); 264 new followers gained across both platforms. These results demonstrate the power of a fully integrated digital strategy, where a strong website and consistent social media presence work together to increase visibility, engagement, and patient connection.
